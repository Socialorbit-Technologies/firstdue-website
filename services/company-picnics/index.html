Company Picnics

The Highlight of Your Company Picnic

A great company picnic needs something everyone can enjoy, from new hires to their youngest kids. A real fire truck delivers. We bring a fire engine and our crew to your park, pavilion or company grounds anywhere in Central Florida.

Employees and families explore the truck, learn fire safety and take a ride, while water games and photo ops keep the fun going all afternoon. It's a simple way to show your team you went the extra mile.

Company Picnics FAQs

Can the fire truck come to a public park?+

Usually, yes. Some parks need a permit for vehicles on the grass, so check your reservation rules.

Will kids get wet?+

Only if you add water games. Let families know to pack a towel.

Where do you travel?+

Anywhere within 50 miles of Apopka, and farther with a fuel surcharge.

How much does it cost?+

All-inclusive events start from $750. Request a free quote for your date.
