Fire Truck Event Services in Orlando & Central Florida

Birthdays, school visits, parades, corporate events and holiday visits. Pick your occasion and we'll bring a real fire truck, a friendly crew and plenty of fun.

Call 321.248.9000

Every 1st Due event starts with a real fire truck, lights and sirens, and an experienced crew who make it safe and hands-on for guests of all ages. Most events include a truck tour, fire safety fun and photo opportunities, and you can add water games, firefighter costumes or hose demonstrations. We serve everywhere within 50 miles of Apopka, FL.
