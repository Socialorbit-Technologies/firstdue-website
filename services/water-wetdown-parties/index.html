Water & Wetdown Parties

The Coolest Fire Truck Water Party in Central Florida

When the temperature climbs, nothing cools a crowd like a fire truck water party. We bring a real fire truck and our crew to your backyard, park or event in the Orlando area and turn it into a splash zone for kids and adults.

Guests jump into water games like bucket brigades and water balloon tosses, explore the truck, and pick up fire safety tips along the way. It's a high-energy idea for birthdays, summer camps, end-of-school bashes and neighborhood cookouts.

Water & Wetdown Parties FAQs

What should guests wear to a water party?+

Swimsuits or clothes that can get wet, plus a towel and a change of clothes.

When is the best time for a fire truck water party?+

Florida's warm months are ideal. We'll help you pick a time of day that keeps everyone cool.

Where do you hold water parties?+

Backyards, parks and venues within 50 miles of Apopka. We travel farther with a fuel surcharge.

How much does a water party cost?+

All-inclusive events start from $750. Your exact price depends on how long you'd like the truck.
