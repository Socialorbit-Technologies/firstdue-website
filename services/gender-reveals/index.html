A Gender Reveal Nobody Will Forget

If you want a gender reveal that stands out from the usual balloons and cakes, a fire truck gender reveal delivers. Our truck rolls up with lights and sirens to set the moment, and our crew reveals the big news with a special surprise.

It's a natural fit for firefighter families, and a showstopper for anyone in the Orlando area who wants photos and video worth sharing. Guests can tour the truck and pose for pictures before and after the big moment.

Gender Reveals FAQs

Is a fire truck gender reveal safe for kids and older guests?+

Yes. Our crew controls the truck and the reveal area at all times.

Where can we hold the reveal?+

At your home, a park or a venue within 50 miles of Apopka. We travel farther with a fuel surcharge.

Can we film the reveal?+

Absolutely. We'll help you plan the timing so your photographer or videographer catches the moment.
