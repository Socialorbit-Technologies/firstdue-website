School Carnivals

The Star Attraction at Your School Carnival

Bounce houses and face painting are great, but nothing pulls a crowd like a real fire truck. We bring a fire engine and our crew to your school carnival, fall festival or field day anywhere in the Orlando area.

Students and families explore the truck, pick up fire safety tips and take photos, while our crew keeps the line moving and everyone safe. It's an easy way for PTAs and organizers to add a headline attraction.

School Carnivals FAQs

How long can the fire truck stay at our carnival?+

You choose. Book the truck for part of the event or the whole day.

Where should the truck park?+

A flat, open area such as a bus loop or the edge of a field works best. We'll confirm before the event.

Where do you travel?+

Anywhere within 50 miles of Apopka, and farther with a fuel surcharge.

How much does it cost?+
