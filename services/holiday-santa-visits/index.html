Holiday Santa Visits

Santa Arrives on a Real Fire Truck

There's nothing quite like the moment kids spot Santa waving from a fire truck. Our holiday Santa visits bring Santa Claus himself, riding in one of our fire trucks, to neighborhoods, schools, churches, businesses and holiday events across the Orlando area.

Santa meets the children, listens to their holiday wishes and poses for photos, while families enjoy the truck and the festive atmosphere. December dates book fast, so plan early.
