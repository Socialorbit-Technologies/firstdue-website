Scouting Events

Hands-On Fire Safety for Your Troop

Scouts learn by doing. A fire truck visit from 1st Due brings a real fire engine and our crew to your meeting place, campout or pack event in the Orlando area, so scouts can see firefighting equipment up close.

Scouts explore the truck, learn fire safety and prevention, and take part in hands-on activities matched to their age. Leaders get an engaging, ready-made program that fits right into a meeting or event.

Scouting Events FAQs

Can you come to a scout campout?+

Yes, as long as the truck can safely reach the site. Tell us the location when you book.

What will scouts learn?+

Fire safety and prevention, what firefighters do, and how the truck and its equipment work.

Where do you travel?+

Anywhere within 50 miles of Apopka, and farther with a fuel surcharge.

How much does it cost?+

All-inclusive events start from $750. Request a free quote for your troop.
