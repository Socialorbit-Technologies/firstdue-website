HOA & Local Community Events

Bring Your Neighbors Together

HOA boards and community organizers know it can be hard to get people out of their homes. A real fire truck changes that. We bring a fire engine and our crew to your clubhouse, park or cul-de-sac anywhere in the Orlando area.

Kids explore the truck and learn fire safety, families take photos, and neighbors finally meet the people down the street. It works for block parties, HOA socials, National Night Out, community days and seasonal festivals.

HOA & Local Community Events FAQs

Can the fire truck give rides to residents?+

Yes. Rides are a favorite at neighborhood events.

Do we need a permit?+

For street events, check with your city or HOA. We're happy to share what we need for parking.

Where do you travel?+

Anywhere within 50 miles of Apopka, and farther with a fuel surcharge.
