Privacy Policy

This notice explains how 1st Due Fire Truck Party and Events handles the information you share through this website.

What we collect

When you request a quote, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email, event type, date, location, guest count and any message.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your request, plan your event and keep in touch about your booking. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

We share your details only with service providers that help us run this website and our business, such as our web host and email provider, or when required by law.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use it. Maps on our contact page are provided by Google.

Your choices

To ask about, update or delete the information you've sent us, email inquiry@firstduefiretruckparty.com or call 321.248.9000.
