Where are you located and what areas do you serve?+

We're based in Apopka, FL, and serve everywhere within 50 miles of ZIP 32712, including Orlando, Winter Park, Sanford and Clermont. We travel farther with a fuel surcharge.

How do I book a fire truck for my event?+

Call 321.248.9000 or request a free quote online. We'll confirm availability and help you choose the right truck.

What happens at a fire truck birthday party?+

A short fire safety talk and truck tour for the guests' ages, a ride around the neighborhood, a chance to put out a simulated fire target, and lots of photos.

How much does a fire truck party cost?+

All-inclusive events start from $750. Your exact price depends on how long you'd like the truck, so request a free quote for your date.

Are your events good for kids and adults?+

Yes. We plan every event so guests of all ages have a great time.

How far ahead should I book?+

As early as you can. Weekends and holidays fill quickly.

In honor of those who serve

Honoring the Men and Women of the Fire Service

Every truck we bring to an event carries the legacy of the firefighters who answer the call, day and night. We share that spirit with every child who climbs aboard, teaching fire safety and respect for the heroes in their own community.

Why "1st Due"? In the fire service, the first-due company is the first engine to arrive on scene. We carry that name with pride.
