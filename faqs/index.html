Booking & Pricing

How do I book a fire truck for my event?+

Call us at 321.248.9000 or request a free quote online. We'll confirm your date, help you choose the right truck from our fleet, and walk you through the details.

How much does it cost to rent a fire truck for a party?+

All-inclusive fire truck events start from $750. Your exact price depends on how long you'd like the truck, so request a free quote for your date.

How far in advance should I book?+

As early as possible. Weekends, holidays, December Santa visits and October Fire Prevention Week fill up quickly.

Service Area

Where are you located and what areas do you serve?+

We're based at 1321 Apopka Airport Road in Apopka, FL, and serve everywhere within 50 miles of ZIP 32712, including Orlando, Winter Park, Altamonte Springs, Lake Mary, Sanford, Oviedo, Winter Garden, Clermont, Kissimmee and Mount Dora.

Can you come to events outside your service area?+

Yes. Events beyond 50 miles may carry a fuel surcharge, and we take trucks to special events across the country throughout the year. Tell us your location and we'll let you know.

At the Event

What happens during a typical fire truck birthday party?+

It starts with a short fire safety talk and a truck tour geared to the guests' ages. Then there's a fire truck ride around the neighborhood, and kids practice putting out a simulated fire target. There are photo opportunities throughout.

Are your fire truck events suitable for kids and adults?+

Yes. We plan every event so guests of all ages, from toddlers to grandparents, have a great time.

Can I customize my fire truck experience?+

Absolutely. Every event is different, so we tailor it to you. Choose from our fleet of fire trucks and add options like water games, firefighter costumes, and fire hose and water demonstrations.

What kinds of events do you do?+

Birthday parties, water parties, school visits, parades, grand openings, corporate events, company picnics, church and scouting events, trade shows, weddings, school carnivals, HOA events, Santa and Easter Bunny visits, Christmas light tours, electioneering runs, ride alongs, gender reveals, movie props and water musters.

Why choose 1st Due Fire Truck Party and Events?+

Real fire trucks, an experienced crew who loves what they do, fire safety built into every visit, and attention to the details that make your event memorable.
